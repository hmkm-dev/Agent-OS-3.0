# Skill: Meta Ads

## Purpose
Plan, audit and optimize Meta advertising campaigns using evidence-backed research, creative variants, budget scenarios and measurable campaign success criteria.

## Allowed tools
`search`, `filesystem`, `playwright` for read/audit workflows. Publishing or changing live campaigns must pass the existing policy and human-approval layers and requires a separately configured Meta API connector.

## Safety
This skill never claims a campaign was published, changed, or performed unless an independent verifier confirms the live Meta state.

## Inputs
Objective, audience, offer, budget, geography, creative constraints and success criteria.

## Outputs
Campaign structure, ad-set hypotheses, creative briefs, budget allocation, measurement plan and verification checklist.

## Success criteria
All recommendations are traceable to evidence; live side effects require approval and independent verification.
